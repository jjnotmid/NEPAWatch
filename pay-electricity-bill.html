Common questions

Do I need to create an account to buy electricity?

No. The whole flow — meter check, DISCO detection, payment and token — works without signing up. You only need an account if you want ongoing power-status reports for your area.

How much is the service fee?

3% of the amount you buy, capped at ₦500. ₦2,000 of electricity costs ₦2,060 total — and the breakdown is always shown before you pay.

What is the minimum amount?

From ₦1,000, or the minimum your specific meter accepts — we show you the exact floor before you pay.

Can I pay postpaid bills too?

Yes. Postpaid bills are credited to your DISCO account within 24 hours; prepaid tokens arrive in seconds.
