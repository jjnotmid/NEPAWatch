2. Description of Service

NEPAWatch is a free, crowd-sourced power outage tracking platform for Nigeria. It allows registered users to:

  • Report electricity outages and restorations in their area.
  • View live reports from other users across Nigeria.
  • Receive free email and push notification alerts for saved locations.
  • Access AI-powered outage predictions based on crowd data.
  • Save multiple locations (Home, Work, School) for personalised alerts.

The Service covers all 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory (FCT) of Nigeria across all 11 electricity Distribution Companies (DISCOs).
